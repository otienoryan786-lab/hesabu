How a day works

  1. Record. The owner logs the payment, or pastes the M-Pesa message and the amount and code fill in.
  2. Check. A driver asks to deduct a repair with a receipt photo. It counts only after the owner approves it.
  3. Agree. The driver agrees or disputes. An agreed day is locked, and a correction is a new record.

What each side gets

Owners

See who is behind today, carry arrears forward, stop one M-Pesa payment being counted twice, and know what each vehicle really earns after repairs and costs.

Drivers

A record nobody can quietly change, a fair way to claim repairs, and a Hesabu Record to show a new employer or lender.
